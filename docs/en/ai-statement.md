# AI statement

The learning harness, the dashboard, this documentation and the research were made with help from **Claude** (Anthropic), in Claude Code.

- **Design and choices:** the learner decided what was built and why, and approved the plan.
- **Code and texts:** largely written by Claude, and read and checked by the learner before they went online.

The harness was made by an AI that limits itself. That is a deliberate choice, but keep it in mind: the research partly relies on Anthropic sources (see §2.0 of the [research](research.md)).
