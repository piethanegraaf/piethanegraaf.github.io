# About

I am Piet Hanegraaf, an HBO-ICT student at Christelijke Hogeschool Ede (CHE) in the Netherlands. I built the learning harness, the dashboard and the literature review on this site, with help from Claude (see the [AI statement](ai-statement.md)).

## Why I built it

On the first test where AI was not allowed, I got stuck. So I looked into whether Claude had undermined my learning, and partly it had. The research shows the same risk: if the AI does the thinking while you practise, you can do less without it afterwards. The harness is my answer: enough help that you do not get stuck, and enough friction that you really learn.

## What I am looking for

- **Educators** who want to try the harness in a programming course. See [For educators](for-educators.md).
- **Researchers** who want to test it, for example in a trial that compares gated AI help with unrestricted help. See [For researchers](researchers.md).
