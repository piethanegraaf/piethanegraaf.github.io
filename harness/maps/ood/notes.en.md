## About this course: Object-Oriented Design in C#

- The learner learns C# and .NET, with NUnit for the unit tests.
- The exercises are in numbered folders (`ood-opdrachten-01/01-…`, `02-…`). `.learning/skills.json` links those folders to a skill.
- **Tooling is L4:** snek, Longboi, SDK versions, Visual Studio, Rider, VS Code, `.gitignore` and `.vs` locks. The guard allows `dotnet --list-sdks` and `dotnet --info`.
- The teacher's files include `.csproj` and `global.json`. Do not change them without consulting the learner.
- Worked examples (L0) belong in `voorbeelden/<skill>/`. You may write there.
